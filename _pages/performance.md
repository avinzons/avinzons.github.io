 As a freelance dancer in New York City, I am so privileged to have performed and collaborated with emerging choreographers, many of them who are also dear friends. Their processes are a significant source of inspiration in my craft, practice, and work as a dancer maker and performer. 

 The works, companies, and choreographers I've performed with have presented at the following venues/festivals in New York, NY: reSOUND at Rockefeller Plaza, National Sawdust (for Hi-Arts Echoes of the Future), TheaterLab, WestFest WestBeth Artist Residence, Judson Memorial Church, Fast Forward at Dixon Place, APAP at Goddard Riverside, Mark Morris Theater (Shared Space Residency and reVENUE), WAXWorks and TRISK Stage Dive at Triskelion Arts, Arts on Site, The Tank (for Diffraction Dance Festival), Hi-Arts, Center for Performance Research (CPR), Common Space at Creature Space, Wave Hill Gardens and Cultural Center.
